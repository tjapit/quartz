title: Externalized Thinking
type: lit
---

Externalized thinking is the result of your thinking efforts *externalized* or
output to an external media. The media can be a piece of paper, journal, phone,
laptop, etc. This is to facilitate thinking and clarify the boundaries of
thoughts, which means that some level of messiness or mingling of ideas is to
be expected, even if the goal is Atomicity.[^1]

[^1]: [🎙️ Zettelkasten — Regaining Depth With the Zettelkasten Method (30:30)](https://youtu.be/F6-_Gr80Pl8?si=LABAufW1a8Y8jPO-&t=1830)
