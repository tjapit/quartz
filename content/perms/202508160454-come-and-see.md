> **Ehipassiko** (Sanskrit: _Ehipaśyika_; "which you can come and see" — from the
phrase _ehi_, _paśya_, "come, see!"). The _Dhamma_ invites all beings to put it to
the test and come see for themselves.[^1]


Belief is overrated. Sometimes, it's a safety blanket that stops further
exploration. As best as you can, always try to look and see for yourself. For
the truth is always there, waiting to be discovered.[^2]
