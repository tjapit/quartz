## 🗣️ Yap

I swear this page was not generated by Gippity, I just really like emojis and icon.
By the way, did you know that the Command symbol (⌘) is called [Place of Interest Sign](https://www.compart.com/en/unicode/U+2318)?

<div class="flex justify-center">

![place-of-interest.png](assets/place-of-interest.png)
</div>


I didn't either until I tried to find it because I wanted to type it in my
Zettelkasten to explain that <span class="keymap">⌘</span> <span
    class="keymap">`</span> (backtick) is,

- the reverse of <span class="keymap">⌘</span> <span class="keymap">Tab</span> when you're already in the window picker popup,
- also, it's how you can shift between multiple windows of the same app.

Pretty cool, right? Have I mentioned [[20240317140859-neovim-and-invoker|my love for keyboard shortcuts]]?

Say what you want about Elon, but I think he got something pretty right when he
first made his BCI company—Neuralink—and spoke on how the bottleneck between
our interaction with a PC is the lag between thought and keystrokes. So, in my
own way, I try to close that gap with keyboard shortcuts. 

Anyways, welcome!
